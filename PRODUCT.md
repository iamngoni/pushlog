# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pushlog serves both people beginning a push-up habit and experienced fitness users who want fast, reliable tracking. Beginners need guided goal-setting, an achievable progression, clear explanations, and encouragement. Experienced users need direct controls, editable targets, deeper history, and the ability to skip guidance.

People use Pushlog in short daily moments, during a live workout, or while reviewing progress. The primary task on every core screen is to understand what to do next and record it with as little friction as possible.

## Product Purpose

Pushlog turns a longer-term push-up goal into a realistic plan and a simple daily practice. Onboarding establishes the user's current ability, desired outcome, preferred schedule, constraints, and coaching style. The app translates that into an adjustable progression, tracks manual or live sessions, and adapts future targets to actual performance.

Success means the user can begin quickly, complete today's work confidently, understand their progress, and return consistently without feeling punished for rest days or missed sessions.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, encouraging, and precise. The core product feels mature and calm, while onboarding lets users choose a quieter coaching style or a more energetic one. Celebrations are brief and earned. Copy is direct, positive, and never shaming.

## Anti-references

- Childish mascots, cartoon-heavy fitness experiences, or imitation-Duolingo visuals.
- Noisy badge walls, multiple currencies, constant confetti, or gamification that competes with the workout.
- Punitive streak mechanics, guilt-driven notifications, or language that frames rest as failure.
- Decorative, poster-like layouts that make core controls harder to find.
- Generic fitness dashboards that foreground every possible metric instead of today's next action.
- Medical or performance claims that the app cannot support with transparent calculations and evidence.

## Design Principles

1. **One clear next action.** Today's target and the fastest way to begin or log it should dominate the daily experience.
2. **Guidance without a ceiling.** Beginners receive progressive disclosure and explanations; experienced users can move quickly and edit the plan directly.
3. **Consistency over punishment.** Reward completed sessions and sustainable weekly consistency. Respect planned rest, recovery, and missed days.
4. **Progress must be truthful.** Show how targets were calculated, distinguish estimates from completed work, and adapt plans from real performance.
5. **Minimal motivation.** Borrow the clarity and momentum of strong learning streak products without mascots, clutter, artificial currencies, or manipulative pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and platform accessibility conventions. Support dynamic text, VoiceOver and TalkBack semantics, minimum 44-by-44-point touch targets, reduced motion, and status communication that never relies on color alone. Provide manual alternatives when camera, HealthKit, or watch capabilities are unavailable. Onboarding and progression must accommodate different ability levels and avoid assuming that every user can train daily.
